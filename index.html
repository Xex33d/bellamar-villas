Where the garden meets the sea air

Bellamar Villas opened in December 2025 in Villa Libertad, El Nido. With just four villas, it stays calm: swim in the pool, eat at the restaurant, have a drink at the bar, and let the day go as slowly as you like. Each villa has its own ensuite bathroom and a comfortable bed.
